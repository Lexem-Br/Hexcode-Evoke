## ✦ Description:

Hexcode Evoke is an expansion for Riprod's [Hexcode](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/hexcode) mod, where you can bring objects to life that will follow you on your adventures and help you automate your home and farms.

  

***

  

## ✦ Hex Creatures:

### ✧ Mannequin:

Give him a sickle to harvest your crops and store the items in the chest, or a watering can to water the plants.

## ✦ How to use:

Hex items can be crafted at the arcane and alchemy bench. To control the Hex Creatures, it is necessary to use a wand.

### ✧ Evoke Book

A simple way to bring Hex Creatures to life, just use Evoke Book on a hex object.

### ✧ Hexcode Gliph

Combine the glyphs projectile → evoke to create an area of effect. Evoke can be created by combining square + triangle + diamond. For more information about the Hexcode system, visit: [https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/hexcode](https://www.curseforge.com/hytale/mods/hexcode)
